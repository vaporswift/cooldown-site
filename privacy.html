Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Cooldown is designed to be private. We do not collect, receive, or have access to any of your data. There is no Cooldown account, no analytics, and no server operated by us.

What Cooldown reads, and where it stays

Claude Code's files on your Mac. Cooldown for Mac reads two kinds of files from the Claude Code folder you choose (usually ~/.claude), read-only:

  • Claude Code's session logs, to count tokens and estimate cost by model, project, and task.
  • A small status file that your own Claude Code status line writes once you add the line Cooldown shows you. It contains your plan usage percentages and reset times, as Claude Code reports them.

Cooldown can only read the folder you pick. You can disconnect it at any time in Settings, and revoke access entirely by deleting the app.

No credentials. Cooldown never asks for, reads, or stores passwords, session keys, or sign-in tokens for Claude, Anthropic, or anything else, and it never contacts Anthropic.

iCloud sync. So your iPhone and widgets can show figures computed on your Mac, Cooldown for Mac saves a small summary (usage percentages, reset times, token and cost totals, recent session and project names) in your own iCloud key-value storage, and a copy in an App Group container on each device. This is your private iCloud, governed by Apple's privacy terms. We have no access to it. Turning off iCloud for Cooldown stops the sync.

Settings such as alert thresholds stay on each device.

Network access

  • Apple — in-app purchases and iCloud.
  • GitHub — about once a week Cooldown for Mac downloads a public list of model prices, so cost estimates stay current. Nothing about you is sent beyond the ordinary request itself.

That's all. There are no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

In-App Purchases

Cooldown Pro is sold through Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment; we receive no payment details. Whether you own Pro is determined by Apple's StoreKit on your device.

Notifications

If you allow them, limit and "back to work" alerts are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Data retention and deletion

We hold no data, so there is nothing for us to delete. To remove Cooldown's data from your devices, delete the app on each device. The iCloud summary is removed when you delete Cooldown's data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage, or turn off iCloud for the app.

Children

Cooldown is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted at this URL.
